# Go-To-Market Strategy

## Target User
Startup founders and engineering managers spending $200–$5,000/month on AI tools.

## Where They Hang Out
- Hacker News, Indie Hackers, r/SaaS, r/startups
- Twitter/X AI tool discussions
- Y Combinator and On Deck communities

## First 100 Users in 30 Days ($0)

| Week | Tactic | Expected Users |
|------|--------|----------------|
| 1 | "Show HN" post | 20-30 |
| 2 | Tweet thread on AI spend data | 15-25 |
| 3 | Reddit posts in relevant subreddits | 20-30 |
| 4 | Cold DM 20 founders with personalized audit | 10-20 |

## Unfair Distribution Channel
Credex's existing customer base and newsletter. The audit is a free lead magnet.

## Week-1 Traction Metric
50 audits completed, 20 emails captured.
