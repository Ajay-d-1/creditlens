# Go-To-Market Strategy

## Target User
Startup founders and engineering managers spending $200–$5,000/month on AI tools.

## Where They Hang Out
- Hacker News, Indie Hackers, r/SaaS, r/startups
- Twitter/X AI tool discussions
- Y Combinator and On Deck communities

## First 100 Users in 30 Days ($0)

Week 1: Post "I audited 3 AI startup stacks and found $2,400/mo in 
waste" on Hacker News Show HN — specific dollar number in title drives 
clicks. Target 30-50 audits.

Week 2: Cold DM the 50 most active members of Indie Hackers who posted 
about AI tools or costs in the last 60 days. Personalized message: 
"built a free tool, took 2 min, found X in savings for a team like 
yours."

Week 3: Post in YC Startup School Slack #tools channel and the 
Buildspace Discord. These are pre-filtered audiences already paying 
for AI tools.

Week 4: Reach out to Ajay (AskAbhi) and Jashwanth (Jaiva) — both 
interview subjects — to share with their intern cohorts. Each has 
15-20 developers evaluating AI tools right now.

## Unfair Distribution Channel
Credex's existing customer list. Every current Credex 
customer is already an AI tool buyer — the audit is a re-engagement 
touchpoint that surfaces upsell opportunities.

## Week-1 Traction Metric
Week-1 traction if working: 50+ audits, 20+ emails captured, 1 
inbound consultation request.
